Setup guide

A handful of one-time steps, then every import is one tap. None of this is required up front - FitDive will ask for whatever it needs the first time it actually comes up.

1

Install FitDive and sign in

Get FitDive from the App Store, open Settings, and enter your MySSI email and password. They're stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device - never sent anywhere except to MySSI itself, exactly as if you'd typed them into their own site.

2

Pick metric or imperial

Also in Settings - this controls the units on the optional Add Details fields (weight, tank pressure, visibility). Everything from the Garmin file itself is unaffected.

3

Set a fallback dive site (optional, but recommended)

FitDive works out your dive site from GPS automatically. For the rare dive with no GPS and no phone location, it needs a fallback site to use instead. Here's how to find a dive-site ID for the site you dive most - your local shop, quarry, or home reef.

4

Sync your buddy list (optional)

Settings → Sync Buddies pulls your MySSI buddy list, with photos, so you can pick who was on a dive from a searchable list instead of typing names. You can also set a favorite buddy that's pre-selected on every import.

5

Share your first dive

In the Garmin Dive app: open the dive, Export Dive as FIT File, then choose FitDive from the share sheet - or pick a .fit file directly from FitDive's own home screen. See Using FitDive for what happens next on each screen.

Already have years of dives on your Garmin? Request a full data export from Garmin Connect, then use Import Garmin History on FitDive's home screen - it finds every dive in the export and checks each one against your MySSI logbook in one pass, so you can see exactly what's already logged and what's still missing.
