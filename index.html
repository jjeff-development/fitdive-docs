Garmin → MySSI, automatically

Your Garmin dive, in your MySSI logbook — in seconds.

Export a dive from the Garmin Dive app, share it into FitDive, and it's logged — dive site, depth, time, temperature, and gas filled in automatically. No manual re-typing.

What it does

  1. Share a Garmin dive .fit file (Garmin Dive app → select the dive → Export Dive as FIT File) into FitDive, or pick one from the app.
  2. FitDive decodes the dive, works out the dive site from the file's own GPS (or your phone's location as a fallback), and checks your MySSI logbook for the right dive number.
  3. If anything's ambiguous — which dive site, or a gap in your dive numbers — FitDive asks you before submitting. Otherwise it just logs it.
  4. You get a confirmation with the full dive summary: depth, time, temperature, gas, site, and the result.

Beyond the basics

Everything a Garmin file can tell us is filled in automatically. For the rest, FitDive has an optional Add Details screen — nothing required, all pre-filled with sensible defaults:

  • Dive buddies — sync your MySSI buddy list (with photos) once in Settings, pick who was on the dive from a searchable list, and set a favorite buddy that's pre-selected automatically every time.
  • Dive discipline, entry type, water body, conditions — Scuba/XR/SCR/CCR/Freediving, shore or boat, ocean/lake/quarry/cave and more, current, surface, and weather — all as quick pickers.
  • Gear & gas — weight, tank type and volume, start/end pressure, gear configuration and details, and an automatic Nitrox check once O₂% crosses 23%.
  • Metric or imperial — pick your units once in Settings; the optional fields follow along.

Track your dives, not just import themv1.1

  • Depth profile graph — every import comes with a depth (and temperature) graph plotted from your dive computer's own second-by-second data, not just the max/average numbers.
  • My SSI Logbook — a real, read-only view of your actual MySSI logbook right inside FitDive: dive #, date, site, depth, duration. No browser needed.
  • Import your whole Garmin history — request a full data export from Garmin Connect and FitDive finds every dive in it, checks each one against your MySSI logbook, and shows exactly what's already logged and what's still missing — even years of dives you never touched with FitDive.
  • Won't double-log a dive — FitDive remembers which dives it's already sent (or you chose to skip), and asks before re-importing one by accident.
  • Backed up to iCloud — your local import history, including saved depth profiles, mirrors to iCloud Documents automatically, so a new device or reinstall doesn't start from zero.
