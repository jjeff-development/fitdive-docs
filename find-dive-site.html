Finding a dive-site ID

FitDive normally works out your dive site automatically from the .fit file's GPS, or your phone's location as a backup. When neither is available, it falls back to a dive-site ID you set once in Settings. Here's how to find one.

You only need to do this once. Pick the site you dive most often — your local shop, quarry, or home reef — and FitDive will only fall back to it on the rare dive where GPS and location both come up empty.
1

Open ScubaGo's search

Go to scubago.com/en/search in a browser (phone or computer both work).

scubago.com/en/search
scubago
Courses ▾
2

Switch the category to Dive Sites

The search defaults to Courses. Open that dropdown and pick Dive Sites instead.

scubago.com/en/search
Courses
Dive Sites
Wildlife
Dive Trips
Events
Centers
3

Find your dive site

Use the search box (magnifying glass, top right) or browse the list, and open the site you want.

scubago.com/en/explore/divesite/white-star-quarry-1965
White Star Quarry
★★★★☆ (11,201)
North America › United States
4

Copy the number at the end of the URL

Once you're on the site's page, look at the address bar. The last part of the URL is <site-name>-<ID> — the trailing number is what FitDive needs.

scubago.com/en/explore/divesite/white-star-quarry-1965
1965 ← paste this into "Fallback dive-site ID" in Settings
Why ScubaGo, not MySSI directly? MySSI's own dive-site search is built into FitDive's automatic lookup already. ScubaGo happens to use the same underlying dive-site IDs and is easier to browse by hand when you already know which site you want.
